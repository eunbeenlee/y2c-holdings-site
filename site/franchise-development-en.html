International Franchise Development

Franchise expansion requires operating partners, clear authority, brand terms and local food supply routes. Y2C focuses on independent business development, partner discovery and supply coordination.

01 / SERVICE SCOPE

Service scope

Support international franchise market entry, qualified operating partners and food supply chain coordination for Korean brands.

  • Compare single-unit and master partner entry approaches
  • Research and preliminarily qualify operators and strategic partners
  • Map importers, cold chain logistics and supply partners
  • Identify country-specific disclosure and franchise legal review requirements
02 / DELIVERABLES

Deliverables

  • Franchise readiness review
  • Operating partner criteria
  • Supply chain checklist
  • Commercial milestones and regulatory review actions

Process

04 / Q&A

Frequently asked

Does Y2C sell franchises or collect franchise fees?

Not under the default development service. Franchise sales activities or fee collection require specific authority and local regulatory review.

Important note

Outcomes, permits and export contracts are not guaranteed. Final scope and pricing depend on written agreements. Specialist advice may be needed for legal, customs and tax determinations.
