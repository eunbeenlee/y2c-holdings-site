Sourcing & Supply Chain

Finding a supplier is only the first step. Y2C brings together manufacturing capacity, MOQ, quality documentation, logistics arrangements and fulfillment responsibilities.

01 / SERVICE SCOPE

Service scope

Source manufacturers, compare MOQ and landed costs, coordinate importers and logistics partners, and plan supply chain execution.

  • Screen prospective manufacturers and production capacity
  • Compare samples, specifications, MOQ and lead times
  • Organize FCA/FOB terms and landed-cost assumptions
  • Coordinate importer, forwarder and 3PL responsibilities
02 / DELIVERABLES

Deliverables

  • Supplier shortlist and due diligence list
  • Quality and origin document requests
  • Quotation and logistics comparison
  • First-order readiness checklist
03 / PROCESS

Process

  1. 01Define specifications and quality thresholds
  2. 02Identify manufacturers and logistics partners
  3. 03Compare samples, costs and terms
  4. 04Document pre-contract risks and actions
04 / Q&A

Frequently asked

Does Y2C always act as importer or seller of record?

No. Sourcing coordination and direct resale are different structures. Contracting parties and import, warranty and product responsibilities are confirmed per transaction.

Important note

Outcomes, permits and export contracts are not guaranteed. Final scope and pricing depend on written agreements. Specialist advice may be needed for legal, customs and tax determinations.
