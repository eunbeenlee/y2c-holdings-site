Service scope

  • Organize FCA/FOB terms and landed-cost assumptions

04 / Q&A

Frequently asked

Does Y2C always act as importer or seller of record?

No. Sourcing coordination and direct resale are different structures. Contracting parties and import, warranty and product responsibilities are confirmed per transaction.

Important note

Outcomes, permits and export contracts are not guaranteed. Final scope and pricing depend on written agreements. Specialist advice may be needed for legal, customs and tax determinations.
