04 / Q&A

Frequently asked

Does an identified buyer mean a confirmed purchase?

No. Discovery, interest, purchasing authority and completed orders are separate stages. We do not represent unconfirmed interest as a signed sale.

Important note

Outcomes, permits and export contracts are not guaranteed. Final scope and pricing depend on written agreements. Specialist advice may be needed for legal, customs and tax determinations.
