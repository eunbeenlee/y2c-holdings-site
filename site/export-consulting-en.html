Service scope

Export consulting and market-entry planning for trade structure, partners, import processes, origin reviews and implementation milestones.

  • Identify FTA origin and tariff review tasks
  • Separate commercial advice from regulated legal, customs and tax opinions

Frequently asked

Does the service certify tariffs or regulatory eligibility?

General reviews identify issues; binding classification, origin rulings and regulatory decisions require authorized authorities or qualified professionals.

Important note

Outcomes, permits and export contracts are not guaranteed. Final scope and pricing depend on written agreements. Specialist advice may be needed for legal, customs and tax determinations.
