Overseas Sales

For companies without a dedicated international sales team, Y2C scopes market outreach, qualified buyer conversations, proposals and practical follow-up. Each project defines deliverables before engagement.

01 / SERVICE SCOPE

Service scope

International sales development, buyer qualification, meeting coordination and B2B pipeline support for Korean suppliers.

  • Prioritize target markets and channels
  • Identify and preliminarily qualify prospective importers, distributors and dealers
  • Support English proposals and meeting coordination
  • Track RFQs, discussions, follow-ups and pipeline stages
02 / DELIVERABLES

Deliverables

  • Target market and customer profile
  • Prospect list with verification status
  • Meeting and commercial pipeline tracker
  • Follow-up action plan
03 / PROCESS

Process

  1. 01Review product, pricing and supply readiness
  2. 02Agree countries and outreach boundaries
  3. 03Research prospects and authorize outreach
  4. 04Report activity and next steps
04 / Q&A

Frequently asked

Do you guarantee a signed export order?

No. Buyer decisions depend on qualifications, demand and negotiated terms. Outreach and execution responsibilities are agreed in writing.

Important note

Outcomes, permits and export contracts are not guaranteed. Final scope and pricing depend on written agreements. Specialist advice may be needed for legal, customs and tax determinations.
