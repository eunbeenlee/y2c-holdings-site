Market Research

Research depth matters. Y2C separates free readiness checklists, modular desk research, practical reports and detailed market-entry studies so buyers can choose an appropriate scope.

01 / SERVICE SCOPE

Service scope

Market research reports covering market size, competitors, pricing, trade rules and entry planning, with clearly scoped research tiers.

  • Identify market size, trends and underlying sources
  • Compare relevant competitors, substitutes and public prices
  • Map buyer segments and distribution channels
  • Flag regulations, certification, HS classification and origin verification needs
02 / DELIVERABLES

Deliverables

  • Scope and data cut-off date
  • Market, competition and customer analysis
  • Source links and confidence labels
  • Go/no-go considerations and follow-up verification needs
03 / PROCESS

Process

  1. 01Define objectives, product and geography
  2. 02Select research modules and validation depth
  3. 03Research authoritative public data and cross-check sources
  4. 04Deliver findings, caveats and next-step plan
04 / Q&A

Frequently asked

What is the difference between the free tool and paid research?

The free tool provides a readiness checklist. Verified market data, competitor comparisons, tariff analysis and economics require a scoped paid study.

Important note

Outcomes, permits and export contracts are not guaranteed. Final scope and pricing depend on written agreements. Specialist advice may be needed for legal, customs and tax determinations.
